Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrackTimer2 ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

TrackTimer2 stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Athlete profiles you create (name and photos you choose to add)
  • Video files you select for analysis
  • Timing sessions and performance data
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

TrackTimer2 uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat may collect:

  • A anonymised app user identifier
  • Purchase transaction data (handled by Apple)
  • Device type and operating system version

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. All payment transactions are processed securely by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Data Storage and Security

All personal data (athlete profiles, sessions, videos) is stored exclusively on your device using iOS secure storage. We do not operate any servers that store your personal data.

Children's Privacy

TrackTimer2 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
