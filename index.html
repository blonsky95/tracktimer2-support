TrackTimer2 Support

Help and information for athletes and coaches using TrackTimer2.

About the App

TrackTimer2 helps athletes calculate speed and hurdles performance by analysing videos, and monitor improvements over time. It offers a free tier for basic use, and a Pro subscription to unlock full features including storing analysed video sessions and computation of best performances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I do with the free tier?

The free tier lets you analyse videos and calculate speed and hurdles performance. Storing sessions and tracking your best performances over time requires a Pro subscription.

How do I subscribe to Pro?

You can subscribe to Pro directly within the app. Go to the subscription section in the app settings and choose the plan that suits you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. To cancel, go to your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, find TrackTimer2 and tap Cancel Subscription.

My video analysis doesn't seem accurate — what should I do?

For best results, make sure the camera is positioned at a consistent angle and distance from the track. Avoid shaky footage and ensure good lighting. If you continue to experience issues, please contact us.

I lost my saved sessions after reinstalling the app — can I recover them?

Pro sessions are stored on the device. If you reinstalled the app, previously stored data may not be recoverable. We recommend keeping your device backed up via iCloud. Please contact us if you need further help.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your videos and performance data are stored locally on your device and are not shared with third parties. Please refer to our privacy policy for full details.
