TrackTimer Support

Help and information for athletes and coaches using TrackTimer.

About the App

TrackTimer helps athletes calculate speed and hurdles performance by analysing videos, and monitor improvements over time. It offers a free tier for basic use, and a Pro subscription to unlock full features including storing video session analysis, PBs and SBs capturing, analytics over time, or computations for best performances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I do with the free tier?

The free tier lets you analyse videos and see timing analysis. Storing sessions and tracking your best performances over time requires a Pro subscription.

How do I subscribe to Pro?

You can subscribe to Pro directly within the app. Go to the subscription section in the app settings and choose the plan that suits you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. To cancel, go to your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, find TrackTimer2 and tap Cancel Subscription.

My video analysis doesn't seem accurate — what should I do?

The precision of TrackTimer is based on the timing between video frames. Any accuracy error is based on the selection of frames and their proximity to the real moment in time. Higher FPS footage will reduce the error greatly.

I changed device and lost my old footage that I had analysed on TrackTimer, what can I do?

TrackTimer stores session analysis and references to videos. If a video that was referenced is no longer present, it will not be usable any longer. However, the session data and personal/season bests will remain in analytics.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your videos and performance data are stored locally on your device and are not shared with third parties. Please refer to our privacy policy for full details.
