TrackTimer Support

Help and information for athletes and coaches using TrackTimer.

About the App

TrackTimer helps athletes calculate speed and hurdles performance by analysing videos, and monitor improvements over time. It offers a free tier for basic use, and a Pro subscription to unlock full features including storing video session analysis, PBs and SBs capturing, analytics over time, or computations for best performances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I do with the free tier?

The free tier lets you analyse videos and see timing analysis. Storing sessions and tracking your best performances over time requires a Pro subscription.

How do I subscribe to Pro?

You can subscribe to Pro directly within the app. Go to the subscription section in the app settings and choose the plan that suits you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them in. On iPhone, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, find TrackTimer2 and tap Cancel Subscription. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose TrackTimer2 and tap Cancel subscription.

My video analysis doesn't seem accurate — what should I do?

The precision of TrackTimer is based on the timing between video frames. Any accuracy error is based on the selection of frames and their proximity to the real moment in time. Higher FPS footage will reduce the error greatly.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open the menu in TrackTimer, choose Backup & Sync and sign in with Google or Apple. Your athletes, sessions and personal/season bests are backed up to your account. On the new phone, sign in with the same account and tap Restore. If you have Pro, it also follows your account to the new phone.

Which sign-in should I use, Google or Apple?

Use the same sign-in method every time, on every phone. Google and Apple create separate accounts unless you link them: in Backup & Sync, choose Add another sign-in method to connect the second one to the same account.

I changed device and lost my old footage that I had analysed on TrackTimer, what can I do?

Videos stay on your phone and are never backed up. TrackTimer stores session analysis and references to videos. If a video that was referenced is no longer present, it will not be usable any longer. However, if you backed up with Backup & Sync, the session data and personal/season bests can be restored on your new phone and remain in analytics.

My Pro subscription is not showing on my new phone.

If you signed in to Backup & Sync on your old phone, sign in with the same account on the new one and Pro follows it. If you never signed in, open the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases while using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your videos and photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. Using the app does not require an account. If you choose to sign in and back up, your athlete profiles and session data are stored securely under your account, and are never sold or shared for advertising. Please refer to our privacy policy for full details.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Backup & Sync and tap Delete account and data. If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion by email. See the account deletion page for the full steps.
